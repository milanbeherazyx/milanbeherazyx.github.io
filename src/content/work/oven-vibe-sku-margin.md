## Context

The Oven Vibe is a D2C bakery built end-to-end by Milan as founder — the analyst and the operator are the same person.

## Problem

A small business needs the same rigor as an enterprise dashboard — sales trends, cost-per-unit, revenue contribution, margin — without an enterprise budget or team.

## Approach

Built the analytics layer alongside the business itself: SKU-level margin tracking plus footfall-density campaign targeting for customer-cluster location marketing.

## What I built

An SKU-level margin dashboard and a location-marketing targeting model, both running on the live business.

## Outcome

Live artifact: [theovenvibe.github.io](https://theovenvibe.github.io/) — a founder-built analytics story, not a hypothetical.
