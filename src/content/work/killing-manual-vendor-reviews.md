At Quinte Financial Technologies, vendor risk assessments were a largely manual, recurring compliance workload across the full third-party vendor portfolio.

Integrated the Venminder REST API, building ingestion and validation for its nested JSON responses.

## What I built

An automated vendor-risk pipeline with SQL validation logic and rule-based monitoring, translating compliance policy into executable rules.

## Outcome

80% of manual vendor risk assessments eliminated; audit scores improved and risk incidents reduced.
